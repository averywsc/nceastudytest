## The big idea
- Simple harmonic motion (SHM) happens when the restoring force is proportional to displacement and points towards <Term t="equilibrium">equilibrium</Term>.
  - **Key link:** SHM is the shadow of uniform circular motion.
- With <Term t="amplitude">amplitude</Term> $A$ and angular frequency $\omega$:

$$x = A\cos(\omega t),\quad v = -A\omega\sin(\omega t),\quad a = -\omega^2 x$$

<summary>Worked example: a mass on a spring</summary>

A mass oscillates with $A = 0.20\ \text{m}$ and period $T = 2.0\ \text{s}$. **(a)** Find $\omega$. **(b)** Find the maximum speed.

1. $\omega = 2\pi/T = \pi\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$
2. $v_{max} = A\omega = 0.20\pi \approx 0.63\ \text{m s}^{-1}$

**Answer:** (a) 3.1 rad/s, (b) 0.63 m/s. **Common mistake:** using $T$ in place of $\omega$.
</details>

## Tips and tricks
- Speed is greatest at equilibrium; acceleration is greatest at the extremes.
